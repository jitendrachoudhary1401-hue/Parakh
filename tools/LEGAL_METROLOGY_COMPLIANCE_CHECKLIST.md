# Project PARAKH — Legal Metrology Statutory Compliance Checklist
**Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA), Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution, Government of India**  
**Statutory Authority**: The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 & Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (as amended)

---

## 1. Executive Overview & Purpose

This Checklist establishes the standard statutory protocol for field enforcement inspectors, nodal verification officers, and commissioners under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (LMPC Rules). Every packaged commodity manufactured, packed, imported, distributed, or offered for sale in the territory of India must satisfy these mandatory statutory verifications before passing compliance scrutiny.

---

## 2. Mandatory Declarations Checklist — Rule 6(1)

Every pre-packaged commodity must display on its principal display panel (PDP) or package surface the following declarations in clear, prominent, and legible form:

| # | Statutory Declaration | Applicable Rule | Minimum Legal Requirements | Non-Compliance Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Manufacturer / Packer / Importer Identity** | Rule 6(1)(a) | Complete registered business name and physical factory/office address with PIN code. If imported, country of origin + Indian importer details. | **CRITICAL (Seizure / Compoundable)** |
| **2** | **Generic Name of Commodity** | Rule 6(1)(b) | Common or generic name of commodity. Where packages contain multiple items, each commodity name and quantity. | **MAJOR (Notice §39)** |
| **3** | **Net Quantity & Unit of Measure** | Rule 6(1)(c) & Schedule II | Expressed in standard metric units (kg, g, L, mL, m, cm, numbers). No ambiguous terms (e.g., "Jumbo", "Family pack" without net quantity). | **CRITICAL (Short measure / False weight)** |
| **4** | **Month & Year of Manufacture / Packing** | Rule 6(1)(d) | Month and year in numeral/abbreviation (`MM/YYYY` or `Month YYYY`). Use-by or expiry date mandatory for perishable food/medicinal goods. | **CRITICAL (Statutory Notice)** |
| **5** | **Maximum Retail Price (MRP)** | Rule 6(1)(e) | Expressed as `Maximum Retail Price ₹ xx.xx` or `MRP ₹ xx.xx (inclusive of all taxes)`. Overcharging beyond MRP strictly prohibited. | **CRITICAL (Section 36(2) Penalty)** |
| **6** | **Unit Sale Price (USP)** | Rule 6(11) | Mandatory for packages $> 1\text{ kg}$ or $> 1\text{ L}$ (`₹ per g/kg` or `₹ per mL/L`). Displayed clearly alongside MRP. | **MAJOR (Rule Violation)** |
| **7** | **Consumer Care Contact Details** | Rule 6(1)(f) | Name, address, telephone number, and email address of person/grievance officer to handle consumer complaints. | **MAJOR (Defective Label)** |
| **8** | **Country of Origin** | Rule 6(10) | Clearly visible declaration of origin (mandatory for all imported commodities). | **CRITICAL (Customs / Trade Infraction)** |
| **9** | **QR Code / Digital Declaration** | Rule 6(1)(g) / 2022 Amendment | E-commerce or digital packaging QR code must lead directly to full statutory declarations without paywall. | **MODERATE** |

## 3. Font Size, Numeral Height & Readability Checklist — Rule 7, 8, 9 & Schedule I

Under Schedule I of the LMPC Rules, 2011, the height of letters and numerals used in mandatory declarations is strictly governed by the net quantity of the package:

### 3.1 Statutory Numeral Height Table (Schedule I)

| Net Quantity (Solid / Liquid) | Minimum Height of Numerals (Normal Cases) | Minimum Height of Numerals (Blown/Formed on Glass/Plastic) | Field Inspection Verification Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$\le 50\text{ g}$ or $\le 50\text{ mL}$** | **$1.0\text{ mm}$** | **$2.0\text{ mm}$** | Optical Caliper / PARAKH AR Vision Sub-pixel analysis |
| **$50\text{ g} - 200\text{ g}$ or $50\text{ mL} - 200\text{ mL}$** | **$2.0\text{ mm}$** | **$4.0\text{ mm}$** | Calibrated Reticle / High-res macro OCR |
| **$200\text{ g} - 1\text{ kg}$ or $200\text{ mL} - 1\text{ L}$** | **$4.0\text{ mm}$** | **$6.0\text{ mm}$** | Standard Digital Rule Gauge |
| **$> 1\text{ kg}$ or $> 1\text{ L}$** | **$6.0\text{ mm}$** | **$8.0\text{ mm}$** | Standard Digital Rule Gauge |

### 3.2 Readability & Background Contrast (Rule 9)

- [ ] **Contrast Ratio**: Mandatory text and background color must have a contrast ratio $\ge 4.5:1$ (WCAG AA equivalent standard for packaging print).
- [ ] **No Camouflage / Concealment**: Mandatory declarations must not be concealed, obscured, interrupted, or distorted by graphics, imagery, or seam folds.
- [ ] **Principal Display Panel (PDP) Proportion**: Declarations must occupy at least 40% of the total PDP area as required by Rule 7(1).

## 4. Barcode, GTIN & Registry Integrity Checklist

- [ ] **GS1 Barcode Compliance**: Product must bear a valid 8, 12, 13, or 14-digit GTIN barcode.
- [ ] **Modulo-10 Checksum**: Barcode must pass mathematical GS1 Modulo-10 check digit verification.
- [ ] **Registry Verification**: GTIN barcode must correspond to a verified retail product in the national commodity registry / Open Food Facts database.
- [ ] **Brand Consistency**: Product brand printed on physical label must match the registered brand in the GS1 commodity registry.

## 5. Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) on Net Quantity — First Schedule

When conducting physical tare and gross weight verification of commodities:

| Declared Quantity ($q$) | Maximum Permissible Error (% of $q$ or $\text{g/mL}$) |
|---|---|
| $q \le 50\text{ g/mL}$ | $9\%$ of $q$ |
| $50 < q \le 100\text{ g/mL}$ | $4.5\text{ g/mL}$ |
| $100 < q \le 200\text{ g/mL}$ | $4.5\%$ of $q$ |
| $200 < q \le 300\text{ g/mL}$ | $9\text{ g/mL}$ |
| $300 < q \le 500\text{ g/mL}$ | $3\%$ of $q$ |
| $500\text{ g/mL} < q \le 1\text{ kg/L}$ | $15\text{ g/mL}$ |
| $1 < q \le 10\text{ kg/L}$ | $1.5\%$ of $q$ |
| $10 < q \le 15\text{ kg/L}$ | $150\text{ g/mL}$ |
| $q > 15\text{ kg/L}$ | $1\%$ of $q$ |

## 6. Statutory Penalties Matrix — Legal Metrology Act, 2009

| Section | Nature of Offense | Penalty for First Offense | Penalty for Subsequent Offense |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Section 36(1)** | Manufacture, packing, or import of non-standard pre-packaged commodity | Fine up to ₹ 25,000 | Fine up to ₹ 50,000 or imprisonment up to 1 year or both |
| **Section 36(2)** | Sale, distribution, or offer for sale at a price exceeding MRP | Fine up to ₹ 5,000 | Fine up to ₹ 10,000 |
| **Section 39** | Penalty for offenses by companies (directors, nominated persons) | Liability attributed to nominated director under Section 49 | Company and nominated director jointly penalized |
| **Section 48** | Compounding of offenses (by authorized Legal Metrology Commissioner) | Sum fixed by Controller / Authorized Officer not exceeding maximum fine | Second or subsequent offense within 3 years NOT compoundable |

## 7. End-to-End Three-Tier Inspection Workflow Checklist

### Tier 1: Field Enforcement Inspector
- [ ] Confirm device GPS fix and automatic address geolocation.
- [ ] Record commercial establishment details (Firm name, Proprietor name, Shop address & landmark).
- [ ] Scan or input retail commodity GS1 barcode with Modulo-10 checksum validation.
- [ ] Capture high-resolution packaging photographs via AR camera viewfinder.
- [ ] Review PARAKH AI OCR extraction and automated rule evaluation.
- [ ] Record inspector field remarks and submit dossier to Nodal Verifier Queue with status `unverified`.

### Tier 2: Nodal Verifier Authority
- [ ] Access dedicated Scrutiny Workspace (`/nodal-dashboard` & `/nodal-verifier`).
- [ ] Examine physical packaging evidence, OCR text captures, and detected rule violations.
- [ ] Add mandatory statutory review comments.
- [ ] Formally execute statutory decision:
  - **Option A**: `Accept & Send to Commissioner` (Status: `pending_e_sign`).
  - **Option B**: `Deny & Reject` (Status: `rejected_by_verifier`).

### Tier 3: Legal Metrology Commissioner
- [ ] Access executive portal (`/commissioner-dashboard` & `/commissioner-portal`).
- [ ] Scrutinize validated dossier and verifier recommendation.
- [ ] Apply cryptographic RSA-2048 / SHA-256 digital signature.
- [ ] Issue formal Legal Notice under Section 36/39 of Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
- [ ] Transmit digital compliance certificate with SHA-256 evidence receipt to manufacturer / retailer.
